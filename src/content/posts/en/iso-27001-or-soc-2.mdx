Founders ask us which certificate to chase first. The honest answer is: whichever one your next biggest customer's procurement team asks for.

## Who asks for what

- **SOC 2** is the default request from US-based buyers and SaaS platforms. It is an attestation report written by an auditor about your controls.
- **ISO/IEC 27001** is a certification of your management system, and is the default in Europe and across most of Asia.

## Where they overlap

Roughly two thirds of the controls map to each other: access control, change management, incident response, vendor risk. Build the shared controls once, and the second framework becomes an extension rather than a restart.

## A sensible order

1. Write down the controls you already operate.
2. Close gaps against the framework your pipeline needs first.
3. Run the audit window while the evidence is fresh.
4. Map the second framework onto what you built.

Skipping step 1 is the single most common reason audits drag on.
