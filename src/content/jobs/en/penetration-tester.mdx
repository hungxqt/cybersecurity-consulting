## What you will do

- Plan and run penetration tests across web, API, network and cloud targets.
- Write reports that engineers can act on without a follow-up call.
- Present findings to technical and non-technical audiences.
- Contribute tooling and techniques back to the team.

## What we look for

- Four or more years of professional offensive security work.
- OSCP or equivalent practical experience.
- Clear writing. A finding nobody understands does not get fixed.

## Nice to have

OSCE, cloud security certificates, and a public write-up or conference talk.
