## What you will do

- Investigate escalations from Tier 1 and decide whether to contain, escalate or close.
- Lead the response to confirmed incidents during your shift.
- Turn what you learn into new detections and runbooks.
- Mentor Tier 1 analysts through weekly case reviews.

## What we look for

- Two or more years in a SOC or incident response role.
- Hands-on with at least one SIEM and one EDR platform.
- A habit of writing down what you found so the next analyst benefits.
- Shift work across a rotating schedule.

## Nice to have

GCIH, GCIA or a comparable certificate, and scripting in Python or PowerShell.
