## Situation

A growth-stage payments company needs a SOC 2 report to satisfy enterprise buyers. A previous attempt stalled: evidence lives in screenshots across six tools, and no one owns the controls.

## Approach

We start by documenting the controls the team already runs, then close the real gaps: access reviews, change approval and vendor risk. Evidence collection moves into the tools engineers already use, so releases are not slowed down. We run a readiness assessment before the audit window opens, so surprises come from us and not from the auditor.

## What this approach changes

Controls have named owners, evidence is collected as part of normal work, and the team goes into the audit knowing where it stands. The evidence pipeline carries over to the next cycle.
