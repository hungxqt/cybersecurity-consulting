## Situation

Depots run on legacy systems that cannot be patched quickly. Logs exist but are reviewed only after an incident, so a compromised vendor account could go unnoticed for a long time.

## Approach

We connect identity, endpoint, email and network telemetry into one pipeline, and write detections for the behaviours that matter in a logistics environment: unusual vendor logins, manifest tampering and lateral movement between depots. Each detection comes with a runbook, so any analyst on shift knows what to do next.

## What this approach changes

Logs are read as they arrive instead of after the fact. Tuning removes most of the noise, so analysts spend their time on genuine incidents, and every detection has an owner and a runbook.
