## Situation

A healthcare provider is building a patient portal on a tight deadline. Security review is scheduled for the end, which is when fixes are most expensive and most likely to be skipped.

## Approach

We run threat-modelling workshops with product, engineering and clinical staff, mapping where patient data enters, moves and rests. The output is a short, prioritised list of design changes tied to specific user stories, plus security acceptance criteria for each sprint.

## What this approach changes

The riskiest flaws, such as an over-broad sharing feature, are designed out early rather than patched after launch. Security requirements arrive as acceptance criteria the team can build against.
